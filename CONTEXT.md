# Curriculum Database

The store of outside, proven educational resources and the curriculum structure they map onto, served read-only to the teacher-facing site.

## Language

**Resource**:
An educational resource that originates outside this project and has been proven against its source. Resources are the only content this database stores.
_Avoid_: material, asset, content

**Proven**:
A resource or claim whose content has been checked against the outside source it came from.
_Avoid_: verified, reviewed, vetted

**Liner notes**:
The accurate notes published with a music video — artist, sources, and the song's classification against outside proven sources.
_Avoid_: show notes, description, blurb
