# Curriculum Database

The store of outside, proven educational resources and the curriculum structure they map onto, served read-only to the teacher-facing site.

## Language

**Resource**:
An educational resource that originates outside this project and has been proven against its source. Resources are the only content this database stores.
_Avoid_: material, asset, content

**Proven**:
A resource or claim confirmed by annotated documentation from the outside source it came from, or by recurrence across independent sources. An unattributed statement is never proof — a document that merely asserts it is the lyrics is not accepted.
_Avoid_: verified, reviewed, vetted

**Liner notes**:
The accurate notes published with a music video — artist, sources, and the song's classification against outside proven sources.
_Avoid_: show notes, description, blurb

**Lesson**:
A teaching plan a teacher builds from a standard curriculum in their school system. Lessons are created by teachers and are not stored in this database.
_Avoid_: plan, unit, activity

**Academic standard**:
An official expectation published by a school system's curriculum framework. The database's job is to surface which standards a song supports, so a teacher can see them while building a lesson.
_Avoid_: outcome, objective, benchmark
